Hands-on tests

Four quick tests for things only you can see, hear or feel. Do them in any order.

1 · Screen: dead pixels and backlight

Fills the whole screen with one colour at a time. Click to move to the next colour; press Esc to stop.

  • Black: look for bright dots (stuck pixels) and glowing patches at the edges (backlight bleed).
  • White, red, green, blue: look for dark dots (dead pixels), stains or tinted areas.
  • Grey: look for uneven brightness or lines.

2 · Keyboard

Press every key. Each one turns green when the Mac receives it. For the top row, hold fn and press F1–F12. Avoid ⌘ shortcuts such as ⌘Q or ⌘W, which would close this page; tap ⌘ on its own instead.

3 · Speakers

Plays tones through each speaker. Turn the volume up to about 75%.

  • Left / Right: the sound should come clearly from that side only. Silence or a very quiet side means a dead speaker.
  • Bass sweep: goes from deep to high. Buzzing, crackling or rattling means a damaged speaker.

4 · Trackpad

Click inside each of the four corner boxes, then press firmly anywhere in the box to test Force Touch.
